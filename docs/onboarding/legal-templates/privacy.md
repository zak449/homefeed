Gwaky is a community for talking about real estate — buildings, blocks,
rumors, hot takes. This page explains, in plain English, what we
collect, what we don't, why, and how to leave with your data
intact. If anything here is unclear, email us at
**privacy@gwaky.com**.

## What we collect

At signup we collect: your email (so you can log in), a display name,
a username, your role (renter, buyer, broker, etc.), and the city or
cities you're tracking. That's it for required data.

After signup we may ask for: your buy/rent intent and timeline, an
optional budget band, a watchlist of buildings, and how you heard
about us. Every one of these is optional. You can skip any question
and come back to it later, or never.

From your activity we learn: posts you've written, comments you've
left, buildings you've saved, threads you've subscribed to. We log
the technical stuff a server has to log to run — IP addresses (hashed
for our records), browser type, and timestamps.

## What we don't collect

We don't ask for your phone number, your social-security number,
your home address, your credit card, your salary, or your real-estate
agent's license. We don't buy data from data brokers. We don't
fingerprint you to identify you across other sites.

## Why we collect it

Your role and your markets are the two levers that make your feed
feel like Gwaky and not a list of every real-estate post in America.
Your watchlist powers notifications about buildings you actually
care about. Your budget band, if you give us one, hides listings
that aren't for you — it is never shown publicly.

Activity data powers comment ranking, the trust score, and the
weekly digest. None of these depend on data we've bought from someone
else.

## What's public, what's private

Public by default: your display name, username, role, and primary
markets, plus anything you post or comment on. You can flip role and
markets to private in your **Profile** settings.

Private by default: your watchlist, your buy/rent intent, your
timeline, your referral source, and your notification preferences.
Your budget band is **never** shown publicly — there is no toggle to
make it public.

## Consent — granular, not bundled

At signup you'll see four separate consents:

1. **Terms of Service and Privacy Policy** — required.
2. **Marketing email** — optional, default off. About one email a
   month if you opt in. One-click unsubscribe.
3. **Personalization** — optional, default on. Lets us rank your feed
   using your role and markets. Off means a chronological feed of
   everything in your markets.
4. **Push notifications** — optional, default off. We ask your
   browser's permission only when push actually helps you (e.g.,
   subscribing to a building thread). Not at signup.

Every consent you grant or revoke is logged with a timestamp. We
keep this log for our records and yours — you can request a copy
any time via export.

## Cookies and similar

We set a session cookie so you stay logged in, and a CSRF cookie to
keep forms safe. We don't set advertising cookies. We don't embed
third-party trackers in pages.

## Who sees your data

Gwaky employees see your data only when needed to support you, fix
a bug, or investigate abuse. We don't sell it. We don't share it
with advertisers. We use a small number of vendors to keep the site
running — hosting (Vercel), database (Postgres), email (Postmark) —
and each one sees only what they need to do their job.

## Where your data lives

Our servers are in the United States. If you're in the EU/UK or
California, the rights below still apply.

## Your rights — and how to use them

- **Access** — you can see everything we have on you. Most of it is
  on your Profile page. The rest is in the export below.
- **Export** — request a JSON archive of everything we've stored
  about you. Usually ready within an hour.
- **Correction** — every field you gave us is editable on your
  profile.
- **Deletion** — you can schedule deletion any time. Your data is
  gone after a 30-day grace period in case you change your mind.
- **Object / withdraw consent** — flip any optional consent off in
  your profile and the matching processing stops.
- **Portability** — the JSON export is machine-readable.

## Children

Gwaky is for people 18 and older. If you're younger than 18, please
don't create an account. If we find an account that belongs to
someone under 18, we delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here, bump the
effective date, and email anyone with marketing consent on. The old
version stays accessible.

## Contact

Questions, complaints, or data requests: **privacy@gwaky.com**. We
aim to respond within seven days. If you're in the EU/UK and want
to complain to a supervisory authority, that's your right too.
