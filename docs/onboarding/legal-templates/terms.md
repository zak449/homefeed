# Terms of Service — onboarding-branch template

> Effective: May 7, 2026

These are the rules of using Gwaky. They're short on purpose. By
creating an account or posting, you agree to them.

## The account

You must be 18 or older. One account per person. Don't share your
login. Don't pretend to be someone else — both impersonation and
using a false identity to gain access are not allowed.

## What you can post

You can post about real estate: buildings, neighborhoods, markets,
rumors, deals, complaints, the whole spectrum. Be specific. Be
useful.

You can't post: harassment of named people, doxxing, illegal
threats, content that sexualizes minors, content that infringes
copyright, paid promotion you haven't disclosed, or anything that
would expose us or other users to liability. We remove violating
content and may suspend or close accounts that repeat the behavior.

## Honesty about your role

If you're an agent, broker, developer, journalist, or have a
material financial interest in something you're posting about, say
so. Disclose it in your profile and in the post. Posting as a
renter when you're actually the listing agent is the kind of thing
that gets accounts removed.

license to host it, display it, and let other users see and quote
it. That license ends when you delete your content (with a short
grace period for caches and backups).

## Our content

Everything Gwaky itself produces — the brand, the layout, the code,
algorithmic features — is ours. Don't scrape it, don't wrap it,
don't resell it.

## Third-party content

Gwaky surfaces user-submitted content. We don't verify every post.
Don't rely on any single post for a financial, legal, or housing
decision. Talk to a real professional.

## Buying and selling

Gwaky doesn't broker deals. Posts about buildings or listings are
conversations, not transactions. If you transact with someone you
met here, you do that on your own.

## Termination

You can leave any time. Schedule deletion in your profile and your
account is gone in 30 days unless you cancel. We can suspend or
close accounts that violate these terms; we'll tell you why if we
do.
